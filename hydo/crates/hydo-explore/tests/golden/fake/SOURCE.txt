The goldens of tests/fake_engine.rs, one folder per scenario.

Recorded in M107 from explore.py at commit 2a58c4b (Python 3.15.0 on the Mac), with 2 workers, on the
scripted engine tests/fake_engine.py of that commit (git show 2a58c4b:hydo/explore/explore.py,
git show 2a58c4b:hydo/crates/hydo-explore/tests/fake_engine.py):

  HYDO_BLESS=1 HYDO_FAKE_EXPLORER="python3 <repo>/hydo/explore/explore.py" \
  HYDO_FAKE_ENGINE_BIN=<fake_engine.py> cargo test -p hydo-explore --test fake_engine

The engine answered every request after a 30 ms sleep: without it, explore.py stops with a
ZeroDivisionError in its summary when the adaptive freeze sweep's two stages take less than 0.05 s
(runs_per_hour divides by their rounded wall time); the answers themselves do not depend on time.

Checked the same day: hydo-explore on the Rust engine (the default run), explore.py on the Rust
engine, and hydo-explore on fake_engine.py all give these files (18 of 18 scenarios each).

Each file is the explorer's output after normalising wall times, worker numbers and the output and
scratch paths; up to 4 KiB verbatim, longer ones as a digest (<file>.sha256: the SHA-256 and line
count, then the first line, line count and a SHA-256 prefix of every 32 lines). summary.txt is the
last line the explorer printed. Since M107 hydo-explore is the reference: HYDO_BLESS=1 cargo test -p
hydo-explore --test fake_engine rewrites them from its output.

M145: the scripted engine is the explorer's test seam `HYDO_SCRIPTED_ENGINE` (src/scripted.rs) instead
of an engine program speaking RUN/BRANCH (both deleted). With the old "by hand" line patched back
in, all 17 remaining scenarios gave these files byte for byte; then they were re-recorded once for
the one intended change: every "by hand" line names `hydo run --image <image> "<schedule>"` (the
engine program's plain `one` mode it named is gone). The `stack_branch` scenario was deleted with
`--branch`.
